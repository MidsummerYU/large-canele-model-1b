# Consent form for annotators (template)

No person annotated the current release; this is the form a human grader would sign before checking model grades.

**Project:** Swiss Newcomer QA, an evaluation dataset of questions on Swiss administrative procedures.
**Team and contact:** Large Canelé Model, via the issues of the project's Git repository.

**What you will do.** Read a question, a reference answer and a model's answer, and mark the model's answer as
correct, incorrect or not attempted. A session takes about 30–60 minutes; you may stop at any time.

**What we record.** Only your grades, linked to a pseudonymous annotator id (for example `annotator-1`). We do not
record your name, contact details or any other personal data with the grades.

**How it is published.** Your grades are published with the dataset under the CDLA-Permissive-2.0 licence and used
to report agreement between human and automatic grading. Published grades cannot be withdrawn once released, but you
may withdraw before publication by contacting the team.

**Voluntary.** Taking part is voluntary and unpaid unless agreed otherwise in writing.
